# Tech Knife - Employee Self-Service Portal Guide

## 1. Overview
Welcome to the **Tech Knife** Employee Portal. This self-service workspace empowers staff to manage daily attendance, request leave, track assigned sprint tasks, view monthly payslips, and access professional development resources.

---

## 2. Navigating the Employee Workspace

### 2.1 Daily Attendance & Check-In
- **Daily Check-In**: Click "Check In" on the main dashboard header upon starting your workday.
- **Biometric/GPS Log**: In remote mode, location coordinates are validated against allowed office zones or home remote profiles.
- **Regularization Requests**: If you forgot to check in or experienced internet downtime, submit an Attendance Regularization request with a brief justification for manager approval.

### 2.2 Leave Application & Quotas
- Navigate to **Leave Portal** (`/employee/leave`).
- View remaining balances for Annual, Sick, Casual, and Floating holidays.
- Click **Apply for Leave**, select dates and leave type, attach supporting documents (e.g., medical certificates for sick leave > 2 days), and submit.
- Real-time notifications alert you when your manager approves or requests clarification.

### 2.3 Task & Sprint Management
- Access assigned deliverables in **My Projects & Tasks** (`/employee/projects`).
- Move task cards across the Kanban board stages (*To Do*, *In Progress*, *Code Review*, *QA Testing*, *Done*).
- Log billable hours against project cost centers.

### 2.4 Payroll & Payslips
- Navigate to **Payslips & Taxes** (`/employee/payroll`).
- Preview and download encrypted PDF payslips for any historical pay period.
- Download end-of-year tax deduction certificates (Form 16 / Tax Statement).
