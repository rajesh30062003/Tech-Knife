# Tech Knife - Customer Portal User Guide

## 1. Welcome to Your Enterprise Client Portal
As a client partner of **Tech Knife**, your dedicated Customer Portal (`/customer/dashboard`) provides real-time visibility into your active software projects, milestone deliveries, budget burn-up charts, and direct technical helpdesk support.

---

## 2. Key Customer Portal Capabilities

### 2.1 Live Project Milestones & Delivery Dashboard
- **Sprint Overview**: Track active sprint completion rates, upcoming milestone target dates, and release candidate versions.
- **Deliverable Sign-Off**: Review architectural blueprints, design mockups, and deployment artifacts. Provide formal digital sign-off directly in the portal.

### 2.2 Invoicing & Payment History
- Navigate to **Invoices & Billing** (`/customer/invoices`).
- Download itemized tax invoices with breakdown of engineering hours, cloud infrastructure pass-through costs, and licensing fees.
- Track payment statuses (*Paid*, *Pending*, *Overdue*) and access receipts.

### 2.3 Technical Support & SLA Helpdesk
- Submit technical support tickets with priority tagging (*Critical/Blocker*, *High*, *Medium*, *Low*).
- Track SLA response times, engineer comments, and resolution timelines.
- Real-time status updates keep your project manager informed of any operational blockers.
